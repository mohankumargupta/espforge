==========================
RMT TX EXAMPLE (ASYNC)

One raw RMT transmit channel sends "hello\n" as a 115200 8N1 pulse train on
GPIO 4: a low start bit, eight data bits least significant first and a high
stop bit. UART1 is the receive side, its RX on GPIO 5 and its TX on GPIO 6, and
diagram.json wires GPIO 4 to GPIO 5. The firmware prints what its own UART
received, so the console shows the text the waveform carried and the example
proves the pulse timing, not just that a transmit finished.
