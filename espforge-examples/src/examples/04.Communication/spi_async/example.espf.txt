==================
SPI ASYNC EXAMPLE

One ADXL345 accelerometer on an embassy board. The generated SPI bus is the
async controller, and the part's endpoint applies its own mode 3 and 5MHz clock
before asserting chip select. Because the driver's bus is async, the sensor is
built after boot: the app awaits `init()` and then awaits each read.
==================

# espforge
  <project name="spi_async" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="embassy" />

# peripherals
  <spi id="spi2" sclk="8" mosi="7" miso="6" mode="3" frequency="5000kHz" />
  <gpio id="gpio10" pin="10" direction="output" />

# devices
  <adxl345 id="accel" bus="$spi2" cs="$gpio10" mode="3" frequency="5000kHz" init="deferred" />
