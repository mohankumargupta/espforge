==================
WS2812 RGB STRIP EXAMPLE

A three-pixel NeoPixel strip driven by one blocking RMT channel. The driver
turns each RGB byte into 24 RMT pulses and latches the frame with a low period.
==================

# espforge
  <project name="ws2812" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <rmt id="rmt0" />

# devices
  <ws2812_rmt id="strip" bus="$rmt0" pin="8" />
