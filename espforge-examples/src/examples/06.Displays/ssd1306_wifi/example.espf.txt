====================
SSD1306 + WIFI EXAMPLE

The shape phase 4 made possible and phase 5 made writable: a Wi-Fi station
needs the embassy scheduler, an SSD1306 needs a blocking I2C bus, and the bus
mode belongs to the bus rather than to the project. The panel shows the
connection state.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="ssd1306_wifi" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="embassy" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="6" scl="7" frequency="400kHz" />
  <wifi id="radio" />

# devices
  <ssd1306 id="oled" bus="$i2c0" address="0x3C" size="128x64" />

# services
  <wifi_station id="home" wifi="$radio" ssid="Wokwi-GUEST" auth="open" />
