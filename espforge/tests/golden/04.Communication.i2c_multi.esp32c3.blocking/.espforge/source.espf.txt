==================
I2C MULTI EXAMPLE

Two different parts on one I2C controller: an SSD1306 panel at 0x3C and an
SCD4x CO2 sensor at 0x62. Each device builds its own `RefCellDevice` over the
one shared bus, so the panel and the sensor never construct a second bus and
never steal the first one.
==================

# espforge
  <project name="i2c_multi" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="blocking" />

# peripherals
  <i2c id="i2c0" sda="6" scl="7" frequency="400kHz" />

# devices
  <ssd1306 id="oled" bus="$i2c0" address="0x3C" size="128x64" />
  <scd4x id="air" bus="$i2c0" address="0x62" init="deferred" />
