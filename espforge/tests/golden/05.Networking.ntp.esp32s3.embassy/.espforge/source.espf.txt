====================
NTP CLIENT EXAMPLE

Connects to Wi-Fi, then synchronises the board's clock once with the `<ntp>`
service. After that the time is read locally — no DNS lookup or UDP exchange
per tick — and the log shows the clock advancing on its own.
====================

# espforge
  <project name="ntp" />
  <chip type="esp32c3" />
  <runtime type="embassy" />

# peripherals
  <wifi id="radio" />

# devices

# services
  <wifi_station id="home" wifi="$radio" ssid="Wokwi-GUEST" auth="open" />
  <ntp id="clock" network="$home" />
