==================
SSD1306 ASYNC EXAMPLE

An embassy project whose panel is driven over an async I2C bus. The device is
initialised after boot (`AsyncDeferred`), lives in an `AsyncMutex`, and
`device!` yields its guard. There is no blocking I2C bus in the wiring.
==================
